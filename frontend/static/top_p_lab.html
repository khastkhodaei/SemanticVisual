SIMULATOR SCOPE

Simulator Scope & Implementation Boundaries

Clarifying what is modeled in this interactive demonstration vs. the complete production protocol.

📚Pedagogical Storyboard vs. Deep Learning Paper Regime Simulator
1. Weight Distributions & Model Dimension: In the 10-node CIFAR-10 storyboard (Steps 1–34), clients hold normalized 10-dimensional class distribution vectors where $P = 3$ ($30\%$ of dimension) with $\theta = 0.70$ is chosen for intuitive visualization and manual verification.
In the Deep-Dive Lab and the real paper regime, models contain $M = 10^6$–$10^8$ weights (e.g. ResNet-18 with $M = 11.7\text{M}$) where $P \approx 10\%–13\%$ achieves $\cos \ge 0.80$.
2. Synthetic Heavy-Tailed Sampling in the Lab: The Top-P Extraction simulation tab synthesizes heavy-tailed weights with latent cluster seeds ($W = \text{bases}[c] + 0.35 \cdot \text{noise}$) to reproduce realistic parameter spectra without requiring local GPU training.
3. Distributed Topology & VRF Integration: The complete multi-phase pipeline (VRF leader election, TTL-bounded zone flooding, inter-zone boundary notifications, and Affinity Propagation meta-clustering) is executed step-by-step in the main application storyboard (Steps 3 through 19).
4. Out-of-Scope Behaviors: As defined in the paper [§2.4, §2.5], dynamic node churn and Byzantine adversarial data poisoning are handled via cryptographic VRF seeds and $(1-q)$ quantile bounds; active malicious packet manipulation is excluded from the normal execution simulator.
